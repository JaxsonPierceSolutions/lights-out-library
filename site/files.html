How to add files

Put them in the files folder inside the project folder, one folder for each kind: for example files/music, files/films, files/documents. They show up here the next time this page opens.

Plays what your browser can: MP3, M4A, OGG and FLAC for music; MP4 and WebM for video; PDF and pictures open in a new tab.
